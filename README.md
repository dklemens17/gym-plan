# Gym Plan

A 12-week, twice-a-week gym logbook for a 12-year-old soccer player: explosiveness, speed, knee stability and throw-in power.

Open it on a phone, pick the week and Day A or Day B, then log weight and reps for each set and tap the check to mark it done.

## What's in it

- **Weeks 1 to 12** in three phases (Learn, Build, Convert). Sets and reps change by phase automatically.
- **Day A:** jumps, hex bar deadlift, split squats, rows, Nordics, Copenhagen planks, single-leg work.
- **Day B:** sprints, bounds and hops, throw-in power throws, goblet squat, bench, pullovers, core, shoulder health.
- **Last week's numbers** show under each exercise and pre-fill the weight when you tick a set.
- **Rest timer** starts when a set is checked off.
- **Knee check** (0 to 10) before each session. A score of 3 or more flags the jumps to skip.
- **Warm-up checklist** with the balance board level.
- **Test weeks** (4, 8, 12): broad jump, 10 m sprint and throw-in distance, compared on the Progress tab.

## Where the data lives

Logs are saved in the phone's browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. Use **Progress > Export backup** now and then, and **Import backup** to move the log to another device.

## Hosting

Served with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root. To add it to a phone's home screen, open the page in Safari or Chrome and choose "Add to Home Screen".
